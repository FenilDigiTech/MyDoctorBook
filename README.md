# MyDoctorBook
    🏥 MyDoctorBook

MyDoctorBook is a smart healthcare platform designed to connect patients, doctors, laboratories, and pharmacies in one place.

The platform makes healthcare services easier to access by providing online doctor discovery, appointment booking, digital prescriptions, health records, laboratory services, pharmacy services, and AI-powered healthcare assistance.

🚀 Key Features

* 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Search & Profiles
* 📅 Online Appointment Booking
* ✅ Doctor Appointment Accept/Reject System
* 🧾 Digital Prescription
* 📋 Patient Health Records
* 🧪 Laboratory Registration & Test Booking
* 💊 Pharmacy & Medicine Services
* 🤖 AI Healthcare Assistant
* 🎙️ AI Voice Appointment Booking
* 🩺 AI Symptom Checker
* 📄 AI Medical Report Analysis
* 📹 Online Video Consultation
* 🩸 Blood & Organ Donor Services
* 🔐 Secure Patient & Provider Accounts
* 👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard & Management
* 📱 Responsive Mobile, Tablet & Desktop UI

🛠️ Technology

The project uses modern web technologies and cloud services to provide a scalable and accessible healthcare experience.

🎯 Vision

Our vision is to build a unified digital healthcare ecosystem where patients can easily discover healthcare providers, book appointments, manage health records, and access healthcare services from a single platform.
